Why the 100-Point Scoring Model Works

A deterministic, auditable alternative to opaque black-box resume filters.

1️⃣

50 Pts: Hard Skill Vector Alignment

Weighted evaluation of the job's prerequisite technologies. A candidate must demonstrate practical proficiency in high-demand frameworks rather than superficial resume buzzwords.

2️⃣

25 Pts: Regional Commute & County Viability

Calculates practical proximity to workplace clusters (Nairobi CBD, Westlands, Upper Hill, Mombasa Port, or Remote). Prevents employer attrition caused by unsustainable cross-county commutes.

3️⃣

25 Pts: Practical Execution & Seniority

Rewards shipping production projects, verified GitHub contributions, and BRS-certified employment tenure. Eliminates credential inflation from unverified certificates.
