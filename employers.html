Verified Kenyan Enterprise Network

Every hiring partner listed on X Power is cross-referenced with Kenya's Business Registration Service (BRS). Job seekers never encounter recruitment fee extortion or fraudulent postings.

🏢

BRS Registry Validation

Automated certificate checks ensure each posting firm is an officially incorporated entity in Kenya with an active corporate registration number.

🚫

Strict Anti-Placement-Fee Policy

Under Kenya's Employment Act, asking candidates for "medical exam fees", "application processing fees", or "uniform charges" triggers immediate blacklisting.

🔒

Data Privacy (ODPC Compliant)

Candidate CVs, phone numbers, and identity records remain locked under the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) guidelines until candidate consent is given.
